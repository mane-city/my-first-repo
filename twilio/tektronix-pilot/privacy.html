SMS Privacy Policy - Tektronix Due Diligence Pilot

Last updated: 2026-04-29

Scope

This privacy policy applies only to SMS/MMS communications sent through the Tektronix Due Diligence Pilot toll-free number (888) 526-6409. Participation in the pilot is restricted to a small group of pre-authorized internal users.

Information we collect

For SMS purposes we collect only: the participant's full name, the mobile phone number used to send and receive messages, the date and time consent was provided, and the contents of messages exchanged through the pilot workflow. We do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, or browser data through SMS.

How consent is obtained

Consent to receive SMS is collected verbally before any number is added to the allowlist. The verbal consent script identifies Tektronix as the sender, describes message purpose and frequency (approximately 2-5 messages per month, only in response to user-submitted requests), discloses that message and data rates may apply, and explains how to reply STOP to opt out and HELP to receive assistance. The participant must provide an explicit verbal "Yes" before enrollment.

How we use the information

Phone numbers and names are used solely to (a) confirm receipt of due-diligence requests sent in to the pilot number, and (b) notify the requester when a report is ready. We do not use SMS data for marketing, lead generation, profiling, advertising, or any third-party purpose.

Sharing

SMS contents and phone numbers are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing. They are processed by our SMS provider (Twilio) solely to deliver the messages described above. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention

Consent records (name, phone number, date, verbal "Yes" log entry) and SMS message logs are retained for the duration of the pilot and for a reasonable audit period afterward, then deleted.

Opt-out and help

Participants may reply STOP to any pilot SMS at any time to be removed from the allowlist immediately. Replying HELP returns contact information for assistance. Standard message and data rates from the participant's carrier may apply.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the pilot SMS program: dkahrs@tek.com.
