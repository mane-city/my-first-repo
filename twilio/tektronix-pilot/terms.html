SMS Terms and Conditions - Tektronix Due Diligence Pilot

Last updated: 2026-04-29

Program description

The Tektronix Due Diligence Pilot SMS program sends transactional messages from the toll-free number (888) 526-6409 to a small allowlist of pre-authorized internal users. Messages confirm receipt of due-diligence requests and notify recipients when reports are ready. The program is not used for marketing.

Consent

By verbally confirming "Yes" when read the consent script, you agree to receive SMS from Tektronix at the phone number you provide. Consent is collected before any number is added to the allowlist. You may withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP.

Message frequency

Approximately 2-5 messages per month, sent only in response to requests you submit. Frequency may vary.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Tektronix does not charge for SMS, but your carrier may charge standard rates for sending or receiving messages.

Help and opt-out

Reply HELP for assistance or contact dkahrs@tek.com. Reply STOP at any time to be removed from the program; we will send a single confirmation that you have been unsubscribed and will not send further messages.

Carriers and delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is on a best-effort basis.

Eligibility

Participation is limited to pre-authorized Tektronix pilot users. The service is not available to the general public, is not intended for minors, and does not contain age-gated content.

Privacy

See our SMS Privacy Policy for details on how we handle phone numbers and consent records.
